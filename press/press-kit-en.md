## Terrasse Season: Montréal Finally Has a Terrace Map

*A free directory of 180 outdoor dining spots across the city, searchable by neighbourhood, features, and hours — launching just in time for the season.*

---

### The Short Version

Terrasse Season (terrasseseason.com) is a free, bilingual web app that maps every terrace, patio, rooftop, and backyard dining spot in Montréal. 180 listings across 24 neighbourhoods. No account needed. No ads.

---

### The Problem It Solves

Every Montréaler knows the ritual. You want to sit outside. You pick a spot. You walk over. It's full. You spend 20 minutes texting friends guesses while standing on the sidewalk.

Montréal has an extraordinary outdoor dining culture, but no central place to find it. Terrace roundups are scattered across a dozen publications, most of them outdated by June. Google searches surface the same 15 famous rooftops. The other 165 spots go undiscovered by most of the city.

Terrasse Season is the answer to "where can I sit outside tonight?"

---

### What It Does

- **180 terraces** across 24 Montréal neighbourhoods, from Old Montréal to Ahuntsic to NDG
- **Interactive map** with a sidebar list — see everything at once, click to explore
- **Filter by** neighbourhood, terrace type (rooftop, patio, backyard, terrace), dog-friendly, covered, heated, and open right now
- **Sort by distance** using your location
- **Live hours** sourced from Google Places, updated each season
- **Bilingual** — full English and French interface
- **Free to use**, no account required

---

### The Data

The listings were compiled by cross-referencing more than 12 Montreal publications: Time Out, Tastet, Cult MTL, Tourisme Montréal, Narcity, Daily Hive, OpenTable, Montreal Diaries, MTL Blog, The Rooftop Guide, experienceoldmontreal.com, and Eater Montréal. Each source was verified where possible. Hours are pulled from Google Places and refreshed before each season.

Features like covered, heated, and dog-friendly are only marked when a source explicitly confirms them. If a detail is not shown, there is no confirmed source for it — the answer is not necessarily no.

### Why Now

The Montréal terrace season runs from mid-April through October. The weeks before opening day are when Montréalers start planning. Terrasse Season launches ahead of the 2026 season to be useful from the first warm weekend.

---

### Community-Driven

Anyone can suggest a missing terrace or correct an existing listing directly through the site. The goal is to make the directory better over time with input from people who know the city.
