# Terrace Season — Outreach Strategy

The goal is to get restaurant/bar owners to submit their terraces to the app, and to build awareness with potential users.

---

## 1. SDCs (Sociétés de développement commercial) — Highest leverage

SDCs are neighborhood business improvement associations. Each one has a direct mailing list of every merchant on their strip. One email to an SDC coordinator reaches dozens of restaurants at once.

## 2. Restaurant & hospitality associations

These organizations reach hundreds of restaurants at once through newsletters and member communications.

- **Association Restauration Québec (ARQ)** — arq.org — Largest restaurant industry association in Quebec. They send regular newsletters to thousands of members.
- **Restaurants Canada** — restaurantscanada.org — National, but has Quebec chapter
- **Conseil québécois des événements écoresponsables** — If you later add sustainability angle
- **Association des hôtels du Grand Montréal** — Hotels with rooftop terraces are a key segment

**Pitch:** Frame it as free marketing for their members. No cost, no account needed, just fill out a form.

---

## 3. Direct outreach to high-value targets

For notable terraces that aren't yet in the app — or that have incomplete info — reach out directly.

**Where to find contact info:**
- Google Maps business listing (most have email or website contact form)
- Instagram DM (many Montreal restaurants are very active on Instagram)
- OpenTable / Resy profile (usually links to their site)
- RestoMontreal.ca — directory with phone numbers

**Priority targets to pursue:**
- Hotel rooftops (they have marketing teams who will want the exposure): Hôtel Humaniti, Four Seasons, William Gray, Nelligan, Auberge du Vieux-Port, Hyatt Centric
- Large-capacity terraces that aren't yet in the dataset: Brasserie Harricana, big canal-side spots
- Breweries — they tend to have big terraces and are community-minded: McAuslan, Messorem, Harricana, Isle de Garde, Vices & Versa

**Email template for owners:**

> Subject: List your terrace on Terrace Season — free
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm the founder of Terrace Season, a new app helping Montrealers discover terraces around the city. We're building a map of every patio in Montreal, and [Restaurant name] is exactly the kind of place we want to feature.
>
> It's completely free and takes 2 minutes: terraceseason.com/submit
>
> We're reaching out to terraces across the city before the summer season starts. Happy to answer any questions.
>
> [Your name]

## 4. Press & media

Getting a single article in a Montreal publication could drive hundreds of submissions and users.

**Target outlets:**

| Outlet | Angle | Contact |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Tastet | They already cover terraces obsessively | tastet.ca/contact |
| Cult MTL | Best of MTL readers would love this | cultmtl.com |
| MTL Blog | English-speaking Montrealers | mtlblog.com |
| Narcity Montréal | Viral local content | narcity.com |
| Montreal Gazette | Broader audience | montrealgazette.com |
| Journal de Montréal | French-speaking audience | journaldemontreal.com |
| Le Devoir | Quality readership | ledevoir.com |
| CBC Montreal | Radio + web | cbc.ca/montreal |

**Pitch angle:** "Montreal finally has a terrace map" — frame it as solving a problem every Montrealer has had. The hook is the relatable frustration of showing up to a full terrace.

## 6. Timing

The terrace season in Montreal runs roughly **April 15 – October 30**.

- **February–March:** Build the app, do initial outreach to SDCs and associations
- **April:** Press push, social launch, "terrace season is coming" angle
- **May:** Peak outreach — terraces are opening, owners are motivated, users are searching
- **June–August:** Ongoing community-driven additions
- **September:** "Last terraces of the season" content angle

The window to get press attention is **March–April** before the season starts. After that, terraces are open and the story isn't as fresh.

---

## 7. Quick wins

- **Leave a card at terraces you visit** — Simple printed card with the submission URL
- **Partner with terrace heater/furniture rental companies** — They know every commercial terrace in the city
- **Contact borough permit offices** — Some boroughs may share their terrace permit list informally even if it's not public open data
